## How it works

Drop or upload an image containing a QR code and it's decoded instantly and entirely in your browser — the image never leaves your device.

## Common use cases

- Reading the destination URL of a QR code before scanning it with your phone, to check it's safe.
- Extracting WiFi credentials or contact details embedded in a QR code image.
- Debugging a QR code you generated to confirm it encodes the expected content.
