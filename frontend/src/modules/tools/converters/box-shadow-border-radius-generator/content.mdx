## How it works

Adjust offset, blur, spread, color, opacity, and inset to build a `box-shadow`, and drag the border-radius slider to round the preview's corners — both update live and the CSS is ready to copy. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Common use cases

- Prototyping card, button, or modal elevation before writing it into a stylesheet.
- Matching a shadow or corner radius seen in a design mock by eye.
- Generating an inset shadow for pressed/active button states.
