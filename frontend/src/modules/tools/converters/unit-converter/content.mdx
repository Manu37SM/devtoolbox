## How it works

Enter a value and pick a category (data size, time, length, or weight) to convert between its units, with a full reference table of every unit in that category shown below.

## Common use cases

- Converting file sizes between MB, GB, and TB with either 1000- or 1024-based math.
- Converting durations between milliseconds, seconds, and hours for logging or scheduling.
- Converting lengths or weights between metric and imperial units.
