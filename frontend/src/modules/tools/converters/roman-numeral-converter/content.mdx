## How it works

Enter a whole number from 1 to 3999 to see its Roman numeral form, or switch direction to parse a Roman numeral back into a number. Conversion happens instantly and validates canonical form, rejecting non-standard notations like "IIII" or "VX".

## Common use cases

- Formatting chapter numbers, clock faces, sequel titles, or copyright years in Roman numerals.
- Decoding a Roman numeral spotted on a monument, book edition, or film credit.
- Checking whether a Roman numeral string is written in valid canonical form.
